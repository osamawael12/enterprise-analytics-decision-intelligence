# Stakeholders

## 1. Stakeholder Overview

The platform serves multiple stakeholder groups across executive,
management, analytical, operational, and technical functions.

---

## 2. Stakeholder Matrix

| Stakeholder | Responsibility | Main Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Executive Management | Strategic decisions | Executive KPIs, trends, risks, recommendations |
| Sales Manager | Sales performance | Revenue, profit, targets, channels, products |
| HR Manager | Workforce management | Headcount, attendance, payroll, attrition |
| Finance Manager | Financial control | Revenue, expenses, budget, profitability |
| Analyst | Analytical investigation | Data exploration, KPIs, root-cause analysis |
| Administrator | Platform administration | Users, roles, permissions, system monitoring |

---

## 3. Executive Management

### Responsibilities

- Monitor enterprise performance.
- Review strategic KPIs.
- Identify business risks.
- Evaluate recommendations.
- Make strategic decisions.

### Required Information

- Revenue
- Profit
- Growth
- Budget variance
- Customer trends
- Inventory risk
- Supplier risk
- HR indicators
- Forecasts
- Business recommendations

---

## 4. Sales Manager

### Responsibilities

- Monitor sales performance.
- Manage sales targets.
- Analyze channels.
- Analyze product performance.
- Identify underperforming areas.

### Required Information

- Sales
- Orders
- Units
- Profit
- Margin
- Targets
- Channel performance
- Product performance
- Store performance
- Customer segments

---

## 5. HR Manager

### Responsibilities

- Monitor workforce performance.
- Analyze attendance.
- Monitor payroll.
- Identify attrition risks.

### Required Information

- Headcount
- Attendance
- Payroll
- Department performance
- Employee attrition
- Workforce trends

---

## 6. Finance Manager

### Responsibilities

- Monitor financial performance.
- Analyze revenue and expenses.
- Monitor budgets.
- Identify financial risks.

### Required Information

- Revenue
- Expenses
- Profit
- Budget
- Actuals
- Variance
- Cash flow
- Financial trends

---

## 7. Analyst

### Responsibilities

- Investigate business performance.
- Validate KPIs.
- Perform root-cause analysis.
- Build analytical datasets.
- Support decision-making.

### Required Information

- Detailed analytical datasets
- KPI definitions
- Historical data
- Dimensions and facts
- Segmentation
- Trends
- Data-quality information

---

## 8. Administrator

### Responsibilities

- Manage users.
- Manage roles.
- Manage permissions.
- Monitor system activity.
- Support security controls.

### Required Information

- User accounts
- Roles
- Permissions
- Authentication events
- Audit logs
- System status

---

## 9. Stakeholder Expectations

All stakeholders should receive:

- Consistent KPI definitions.
- Trusted data.
- Role-appropriate access.
- Actionable insights.
- Transparent analytical logic.
- Reliable reporting.
