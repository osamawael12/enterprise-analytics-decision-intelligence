# Business Scenario

## 1. Overview

The Enterprise Analytics & Decision Intelligence Platform is designed for a fictional
multi-domain enterprise organization operating across multiple regions, stores,
sales channels, suppliers, warehouses, and corporate departments.

The organization generates large volumes of operational data across Sales, Customers,
Products, Inventory, Procurement, Suppliers, HR, Finance, Marketing, Stores, and Geography.

The organization currently faces challenges caused by fragmented data sources,
inconsistent reporting, limited visibility into business performance, and slow
decision-making processes.

The proposed platform provides a centralized analytical ecosystem that transforms
operational data into trusted KPIs, insights, predictions, recommendations, and
automated business actions.

---

## 2. Business Vision

The vision is to establish a unified enterprise analytics platform that enables
decision-makers to move from:

Operational Data
→ Trusted Data
→ Analytics
→ Insights
→ Predictions
→ Recommendations
→ Decisions
→ Automated Actions

---

## 3. Current-State Challenges

The organization experiences several analytical challenges:

- Data is distributed across multiple operational sources.
- Business teams rely heavily on manual reporting.
- KPI definitions may differ between departments.
- Historical performance is difficult to analyze consistently.
- Management lacks a unified view of business performance.
- Root-cause analysis is time-consuming.
- Predictive capabilities are limited.
- Business recommendations are not systematically connected to analytical results.
- Data quality issues may remain undetected.
- Reporting and alerting processes are partially manual.

---

## 4. Proposed Future State

The platform will provide:

- A governed SQL Server data warehouse.
- Reusable Python ETL/ELT pipelines.
- Automated data-quality validation.
- Analytical SQL datasets.
- Excel management reporting.
- Enterprise Power BI dashboards.
- FastAPI backend services.
- Secure web and Streamlit applications.
- Machine learning capabilities.
- AI-powered business analysis.
- Decision intelligence capabilities.
- What-If scenario analysis.
- n8n workflow automation.
- Automated reports and alerts.

---

## 5. Decision Intelligence Vision

The platform should answer four levels of business questions:

### Descriptive

What happened?

### Diagnostic

Why did it happen?

### Predictive

What is likely to happen next?

### Prescriptive

What should the business do?

The final platform extends this model by connecting recommendations to
automated actions.
