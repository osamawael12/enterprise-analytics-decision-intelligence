# User Personas

## 1. Executive Persona

### Role

Executive

### Primary Goal

Understand overall enterprise performance and make strategic decisions.

### Main Questions

- Are we meeting our targets?
- Where are we underperforming?
- What are the biggest risks?
- What should management do next?

### Platform Needs

- Executive dashboard
- KPI cards
- Trends
- Alerts
- Forecasts
- Recommendations
- Decision Center

---

## 2. Sales Manager Persona

### Role

Sales Manager

### Primary Goal

Improve sales performance and profitability.

### Main Questions

- Which products perform best?
- Which stores are underperforming?
- Which channels are growing?
- Where are margins declining?

### Platform Needs

- Sales dashboard
- Product analysis
- Store analysis
- Channel analysis
- Customer segmentation
- Forecasting

---

## 3. HR Manager Persona

### Role

HR Manager

### Primary Goal

Improve workforce management and identify workforce risks.

### Main Questions

- How is headcount changing?
- Which departments have high attrition?
- What attendance patterns exist?
- Which employees or departments may require attention?

### Platform Needs

- HR dashboard
- Attendance analytics
- Payroll analytics
- Attrition prediction
- Workforce trends

---

## 4. Finance Manager Persona

### Role

Finance Manager

### Primary Goal

Control financial performance and identify financial risks.

### Main Questions

- Are actual results aligned with budget?
- Where are expenses increasing?
- Which areas generate the most profit?
- What financial risks require attention?

### Platform Needs

- Finance dashboard
- Budget vs Actual
- Profitability analysis
- Cash flow analysis
- Forecasting
- Alerts

---

## 5. Analyst Persona

### Role

Data Analyst

### Primary Goal

Investigate business problems and produce reliable analytical insights.

### Main Questions

- Why did a KPI change?
- Which factors explain the change?
- Which segments are driving performance?
- What data-quality issues exist?

### Platform Needs

- Analytical datasets
- SQL views
- Detailed filters
- Drill-down
- Data-quality dashboard
- Export capabilities
- Python analytics

---

## 6. Administrator Persona

### Role

Platform Administrator

### Primary Goal

Maintain secure and reliable platform access.

### Main Questions

- Who has access?
- Which roles are assigned?
- Are authentication events normal?
- Are there security issues?

### Platform Needs

- User management
- Role management
- Permission management
- Audit logs
- Security monitoring
