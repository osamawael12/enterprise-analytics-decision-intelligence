# Business Questions

## 1. Purpose

The platform must answer business questions across descriptive,
diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics.

---

# Sales Questions

### Descriptive

1. What is total revenue?
2. What is total profit?
3. How many orders were generated?
4. How many units were sold?
5. How are sales trending over time?
6. Which channels generate the most revenue?
7. Which stores perform best?
8. Which products generate the highest sales?

### Diagnostic

9. Why did sales increase or decrease?
10. Which products are driving sales changes?
11. Which regions are driving performance?
12. Which channels are underperforming?
13. Which stores are below target?

### Predictive

14. What are expected future sales?
15. Which products are likely to experience demand changes?
16. Which stores are likely to underperform?

### Prescriptive

17. Which sales areas should management prioritize?
18. Which products require attention?
19. Which stores require corrective action?
